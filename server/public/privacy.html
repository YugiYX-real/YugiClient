Privacy

Last updated 7 August 2026

The short version: we keep the least we can, we do not sell anything to anyone, and there are no third party trackers or advertising scripts on this site.

What we store when you register

  • Your username and email address.
  • Your password, only as a scrypt hash with a random salt. The password itself is never written down and cannot be recovered from what we hold.
  • The Minecraft name you choose to link, if you link one.
  • When the account was created and when it last signed in.

What we store when you play

  • Your in game name, the client version and the time of the last heartbeat, so the online counter and the player badge work.
  • Which cosmetics you were granted and which one you are wearing.

Presence records are deleted automatically once an account has not been seen for seven days.

Cookies

One cookie, named halcyon_session, which holds a random session token after you sign in. It is set to HttpOnly and SameSite, it expires after thirty days, and it exists only to keep you signed in. There are no analytics or advertising cookies, so there is no consent banner to click away.

Microsoft sign in

The launcher signs in to Minecraft through Microsoft. That exchange happens between your computer and Microsoft. Your Microsoft password never reaches us, and the resulting session token is stored on your own machine, not on this server.

Server logs

The service keeps short lived operational logs, which can include ip addresses, for security and debugging. They are not used to build profiles.

Who else sees it

Nobody. Data lives on a single server rented in Germany and is not shared, sold, or handed to advertisers. The launcher does contact Microsoft, Mojang, Modrinth and GitHub while it works, because that is where accounts, mods and releases come from.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. Deleting your account removes your login, your linked name and your cosmetic grants. Write to the address on the imprint page and we will act within a month.
