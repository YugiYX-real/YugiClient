Create a cosmetic

Capes, wings, hats and anything else, still or animated

The easy way in: export whatever you drew as a gif and drop it here. The server takes it apart into one png per frame, reads the speed out of the gif itself and keeps the gif so the website can play it. Transparency is kept, up to 64 frames, and a longer gif is thinned out evenly rather than cut short.
A cape is one 64x32 sheet. Leave this empty when you upload a gif or frames, and the first frame becomes the still picture on its own.
Pick every frame at once, named so they sort in the order they should play, for example frame-01.png to frame-12.png. Frames picked here win over a gif.
Leave those five alone and the kind decides: wings stand off the back as a mirrored pair and beat as you move, a halo floats above the head and glows, a cape hangs flat. Fill one in when a particular piece of art needs to sit a little bigger, higher or further out than the rest of its kind.
