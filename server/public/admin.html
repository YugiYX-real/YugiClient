Create a cosmetic

Capes, wings, hats and anything else, still or animated

A cape is one 64x32 sheet. Leave this empty if you are uploading frames below, and the first frame becomes the still picture on its own.
Pick every frame at once, named so they sort in the order they should play, for example frame-01.png to frame-12.png. Each one is stored beside the cosmetic and the client plays them at the speed above, in the world as well as in the wardrobe. This is the way to animate wings or a cape with art of your own; a stacked strip is only there for the older ones. Up to 64 frames.
